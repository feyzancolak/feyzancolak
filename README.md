## 👨‍💻 About Me  

I am an AI & Software Engineer with a strong focus on **artificial intelligence and data engineering**, bringing together analytical thinking and creative expression. I graduated with a **Master’s in Artificial Intelligence & Data Engineering** from the University of Pisa, after earning two Bachelor’s degrees in **Software Engineering** and **Visual Communication & Design**. My academic journey gave me a solid foundation in **deep learning, machine learning, and big data processing**, while also sharpening my ability to communicate complex ideas clearly and effectively.  

My primary interests lie in **computer vision, large language models, and scalable machine learning systems**, and I enjoy applying these technologies to solve **real-world challenges**. I specialize in building **intelligent applications** that transform research into practical, measurable outcomes. I am passionate about **cloud technologies, big data management, and computational intelligence**, and I continuously expand my expertise in these areas to stay at the forefront of innovation.  

While I maintain a creative edge through **design and visual communication**, my professional focus is on **advancing AI-driven solutions and data engineering practices**. I value **organization, collaboration, and adaptability**, especially in international and multidisciplinary environments. Ultimately, my goal is to contribute to the development of **scalable, intelligent systems** that make **complex data and advanced AI technologies accessible, impactful, and transformative — enriched with a touch of creativity to deliver solutions that are both effective and engaging**.
